# Implicit composite non-ideal MHD diffusion (`nimhd_solver='implicit'`)

Design contract for the GPU implicit Ohmic + ambipolar solver. The Python
reference (outside the repository) and the CUDA Fortran implementation follow
the same conventions. Notation and the mathematical rationale follow the
vault handoff "Composite AMR implicit diffusion — design and implementation
handoff" (2026-10-05).

## 1. Scope

- 3D, `MHD`, CUDA, one rank, periodic box, `nsubcycle=1` on all levels
  (common time step). Uniform grids and AMR.
- The mesh must satisfy condition **P**: every cell with grandchildren has all
  26 same-level neighbours refined. P is checked at every topology build; a
  violation aborts the run.
- Split placement is that of the composite explicit/STS solver
  (`nimhd_composite`): Strang halves before the Godunov steps (at the finest
  level, once the time step is known) and after the restriction at levelmin,
  or one Godunov-split call after the restriction.
- The solver works in `real(kind=8)` regardless of `NPRE`.

## 2. Mesh conventions

- Level `l` cells have width `h_l = boxlen/2**l`. An oct of level `l` with
  Cartesian key `k` holds the level-`l` cells with integer coordinates
  `c = 2k + bit`, `bit=(i,j,k)` in `{0,1}^3`, `cell_idx = 1+i+2j+4k`.
- Periodic wrap at level `l` uses `[2*box_ckey_min(d,l), 2*box_ckey_max(d,l))`.
- The oct of level `l` and key `k` is found with
  `hash_get(key_off(l) + kx + ky*nx + kz*nx*nx)`, `nx = ckey_max(l)`.
  An oct is **real** iff `1 <= index <= ngridmax` (cache/ghost octs have
  larger indices and are never physical).
- A cell `(l,c)` **exists** iff its oct `(l, c>>1)` is real; it is
  **refined** iff the oct `(l+1, c)` is real; a **leaf** exists and is not
  refined. Leaves are at levels `levelmin..lf`.

## 3. Physical entities and ownership

Physical entities are the faces and edge segments of the leaf complex, oriented
along `+axis`. Integer lattice coordinates at level `L` locate them.

- **Face** `(L, a, p)`: normal `a`, lower corner lattice point `p`, between the
  level-`L` cells `p - e_a` (minus side) and `p` (plus side). It is physical at
  level `L` iff neither of these cells is refined and at least one is a leaf.
  Owner: the plus-side cell if it is a leaf at level `L`, otherwise the minus
  side. The owner's face slot (`a+1` for its minus face, `a+4` for its plus
  face) is exactly this face.
- **Edge** `(L, a, p)`: along `a`, from lattice point `p` to `p+e_a`. The four
  level-`L` cells around it are `p - d1*e_b - d2*e_c`, `b<c` the other two axes,
  in the canonical order `(d1,d2) = (0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)`. It is physical
  at level `L` iff none of them is refined and at least one is a leaf. Owner:
  the first leaf at level `L` in canonical order.
- Each leaf counts its owned faces and edges; exclusive prefix sums give the
  1-based ids `1..nface`, `1..nedge`. Lookups resolve an entity through its
  owner's slot.
- **Incidence** `C(f,e)` (face rows): for face `(L,a,p)`, `b=(a+1)%3`,
  `c=(a+2)%3`, the boundary sides are `+edge_b at p`, `+edge_c at p+e_b`,
  `-edge_b at p+e_c`, `-edge_c at p`. Each side of length `h_L` is either one
  physical edge at level `L` or two halves at level `L+1` (never finer, by P).
  The transpose is built as an explicit CSR (edge rows), so that `C` and `C^T`
  are both gathers. Orientation is the right-hand rule about `+e_a`.
- Integrated unknowns: `b_f = A_f B_f` (`A_f=h_L^2`), `e_i = h_L E_i`.
  `D C = 0` holds exactly in integers.

## 4. Oct reference element

On an oct (or a leaf treated as a virtual oct) of half-width `h`, centre `x_c`,
`s = (x-x_c)/h` in `[-1,1]^3`. 1D bases:

    nodal  l_0=s(s-1)/2, l_1=1-s^2, l_2=s(s+1)/2      (nodes s=-1,0,1)
    interval a_0=(1-2s)/2, a_1=(1+2s)/2              (unit integral on [-1,0], [0,1])

Local entities of the 2x2x2 subdivision (0-based, this ordering is binding):

- edges `e = 18a + 9i + 3j + k`: `p[a]=i in {0,1}`, `p[o0]=j`, `p[o1]=k` in
  `{0,1,2}`, `(o0,o1)` the other axes in increasing order;
- faces `f = 12a + 4i + 2j + k`: `p[a]=i in {0,1,2}`, `p[b]=j`, `p[c]=k` in
  `{0,1}`, `b=(a+1)%3`, `c=(a+2)%3`;
- vertices `v = 9p0 + 3p1 + p2`, `p in {0,1,2}^3`.

Fields: `B_a(x) = h^-2 sum_{f: axis a} u_f prod_d phi_d(s_d)` with
`phi_d = nodal_{p[d]}` if `d==a` else `interval_{p[d]}`;
`E_a(x) = h^-1 sum_{e: axis a} u_e prod_d psi_d(s_d)` with
`psi_d = interval_{p[d]}` if `d==a` else `nodal_{p[d]}`.

Reference tables (computed at start-up from these formulas, except the lift
pseudo-inverses, which are generated by `utils/py/implicit_tables.py`):

- `MF_ref(f,g) = delta(a_f,a_g) prod_d M_d(p_f[d],p_g[d])`, `M_d = M0` if
  `d==a` else `M1`; `ME_ref` with the roles exchanged;
  `M0 = [[4,2,-1],[2,16,2],[-1,2,4]]/15`, `M1 = [[7,-1],[-1,7]]/6`.
  Leaf masses: `M_F,K = (2/w) MF_ref`, `M_E,K = (w/2) ME_ref`, `w` the leaf width.
- `RF(bit)`, `RE(bit)` (36x36, 54x54): exact sub-integrals of the parent oct
  polynomial over the child's virtual entities, as `face_restriction` and
  `restriction` in the host fixtures (lower corner `bit - 1 + p/2` in parent
  `s` units, nodal values in the "point" directions, interval integrals over
  length 1/2 in the others).
- Gauss tables: 3 and 5 points per axis on `[-1,1]`.

## 5. Reconstruction of every leaf

For a leaf `K` (width `w`, parent oct `T`, child position `bit`):

    v_F = R_T,F b   (36)     v_E = R_T,E e   (54)

`R_T` sums the physical descendants of each skeleton entity of `T` (CSR per
contributing oct; up to 16 faces / 4 segments per row under P). Then

    u_F,K = RF(bit) v_F + [transition] Lambda_F nu
    u_E,K = RE(bit) v_E + [transition] Lambda_E tau(d)

and the fields on `K` are the reference fields of section 4 with `h=w/2`.

A leaf is a **transition** leaf if any face (`sf`, 6 flags) or edge (`se`,
12 flags; `se` implies nothing about faces, a split face implies its four
perimeter edges are split) is split by a finer neighbour. For it the topology
stores the physical ids of the 24 boundary virtual faces (`bf`, those with
`p[a] in {0,2}`, in local order) and of the 48 boundary virtual edges (`be`)
that are physical, 0 otherwise.

- Faces: `nu_r = b_phys(r) - (RF(bit) v_F)_r` on split faces, 0 elsewhere;
  `Lambda_F = C_ref[:,be] pinv(C_b)` (36x24).
- Edges: `d = e_phys - (RE(bit) v_E)` on physical boundary virtual edges.
  Potentials on the 27 virtual vertices: on a split edge, the midpoint gets
  `d` of its first half; each face centre gets the mean of its four edge
  midpoints; corners and the cell centre 0. `tau = G_b phi`, then on every
  split face `tau = d` on the 12 boundary virtual edges lying in that face.
  `Lambda_E tau = G chi + I_b z`, `z = pinv(C_b) C_b tau`,
  `chi_b = pinv(G_b)(tau - z)`, `chi_centre` = mean of the six face-centre
  `chi`. `Lambda_E` (54x48) is a fixed table.

These maps satisfy `R_F L_F = I`, `R_E L_E = I` and `curl L_E = L_F C` on
every leaf; the in-situ check (`nimhd_check`) verifies them on the run mesh
with random vectors, together with the adjoint identities of every transpose.

## 6. Operators

    M_F = sum_K L_F,K^T M_F,K L_F,K      M_E = sum_K L_E,K^T M_E,K L_E,K
    K(W) = sum_K L_E,K^T [ sum_q w_q (w/2)^3 Psi_q^T T_q Psi_q ] L_E,K
    T_q = eta_o I + eta_a (|B_q|^2 I - B_q B_q^T),  B_q = L_F,K W at q

5x5x5 Gauss points per leaf for `K` when `eta_a` is nonzero anywhere; for pure
Ohmic runs `K = sum_K eta_o,K L^T M_E,K L` exactly. `eta_o,K, eta_a,K` come from
`nimhd_coeff(uu,bb,...)` per leaf with `uu=uold` (density floored to `smallr`)
and `bb` the opposite-face average of the native leaf faces of `W`. No floor,
cap or Ohmic regularisation is applied; negative or non-finite coefficients
abort the run.

    J = M_E^-1 C^T M_F b,   E = M_E^-1 K J,   H b = C E.

`M_E^-1` is applied by Jacobi-preconditioned CG to a relative residual `1e-13`
(true residual monitored).

## 7. Time step (Pade(0,2), common mobility)

With `a = dt/2`, `F_W(B) = -H(W) B`: `X = Y + dt F_W(W)`, `W = X - a F_W(X)`.
For frozen `H` this is `(I + dt H + dt^2 H^2/2) X = Y`, solved through the
complex shift `sigma = 1 - i`:

    (sigma I + dt H) Z = Y,   U = 2 Re Z,  X = 2 Im Z,  W = Re Z + Im Z

with COCG in the `M_F` bilinear form (unconjugated), to
`||r||_MF <= nimhd_rtol ||Y||_MF`. For ambipolar diffusion (or field-dependent
coefficients) Picard iterates the mobility: `K_k = K(W_k)`, solve, set
`W_{k+1}`, until `||W_{k+1}-W_k||_MF <= nimhd_rtol ||Y||_MF`.

Commit: `J_s = M_E^-1 C^T M_F s`, `E_s = M_E^-1 K(W) J_s` for `s=U,X`;

    B_U = Y - a C (E_U - E_X),   X_acc = Y - a C (E_U + E_X).

`X_acc` is the accepted field (exact CT). The endpoint mismatch
`||X_acc - X||_MF/||Y||_MF` must be below `100*nimhd_rtol`.

## 8. Energy

Per leaf `c` and stage `s`: `Pi_c,s = (C E_s)^T M_F,c B_s`,
`q_c,s = J_s^T K_c J_s`, `rho_c,s = sum_{edges i owned by c}
(J_i r_E,i - E_i r_J,i)` with `r_J = M_E J_s - C^T M_F B_s`,
`r_E = M_E E_s - K J_s`. Then

    dE_tot,c = -a sum_s (Pi_c,s - q_c,s - rho_c,s)

which sums to zero over the leaves for any `(J,E)` and equals
`m_c(X_acc) - m_c(Y) + heat_c`, `heat_c = a sum_s (q+rho) + (a^2/2)
||C(E_U-E_X)||^2_{M_F,c}`. Total energy density changes by `dE_tot,c/w^3`.
The raw pressure of every leaf after the update, from the native
opposite-face magnetic energy and without floors, must be positive.
Isothermal and induction runs skip the energy update.

## 9. Acceptance and rejection

A step is accepted if every Krylov/CG solve and the Picard iteration converge,
the endpoint mismatch is below tolerance and every raw pressure is positive.
Otherwise the pre-step `bold` and `uold` are restored and the interval is
split in two and then four equal steps (`nimhd_maxsplit=2` levels). Exhaustion
aborts the run; no partial step is accepted.

## 10. Native transfer

- Gather: `b_f = A_f * bold(owner slot)`. Every other native copy (the
  neighbour's slot, and the coarse leaf slot of a split face, which must equal
  the average of its four fine fragments) is compared with the physical value
  in the check mode.
- Scatter: every leaf slot gets `sum of its physical fragments / w^2`. The
  refined cells are then restricted from the finest level down
  (`nimhd_restrict_b_kernel`) and the ghost octs are refreshed from the
  coarser level, as in the composite STS path; `uold` is then uploaded.

## 11. Parameters

- `nimhd_solver='implicit'`
- `nimhd_rtol` (default `1d-8`): Krylov and Picard tolerance.
- `nimhd_maxiter` (default `1000`): maximum COCG / inner CG / Picard iterations.
- `nimhd_check` (default `.false.`): run the identity and native-copy checks at
  every topology build and report the residuals.

## 12. Code organisation (binding interfaces)

Files, in build order after `gpu_hydro.o gpu_refine.o`:

1. `gpu/gpu_implicit_tables.f90` — module `implicit_tables`, generated by
   `utils/py/implicit_tables.py`: `real(kind=8), parameter :: lift_f(36,24),
   lift_e(54,48)` with `lift_f(i,r) = Lambda_F[i-1,r-1]` (local orders of
   section 4; `r` runs over `bf`/`be` in increasing local index).
2. `gpu/gpu_implicit_ops.cuf` — module `implicit_ops`: topology, native
   transfer and operator actions. All vectors are `real(kind=8), device`.
   Public state (host scalars): `imp_nleaf, imp_nface, imp_nedge, imp_ntr`;
   device arrays `imp_leaf_oct(:), imp_leaf_cell(:), imp_leaf_lev(:)`,
   `imp_eta(2,:)` (eta_o, eta_a per leaf, filled by the solver),
   host logical `imp_quad` (use the 5-point mobility).

       subroutine imp_build(grid, hash_key, hash_val, hash_size, ckey_max, key_off,
                            box_ckey_min, box_ckey_max, head, noct, ngridmax,
                            lmin, lf, boxlen, info)
       subroutine imp_gather(bold, b)            ! physical face fluxes from leaf slots
       subroutine imp_scatter(b, bold)           ! every leaf slot from b
       subroutine imp_leaf_bc(b, bc)             ! bc(3,nleaf): native cell-centred B
       subroutine imp_leaf_slots(b, bs)          ! bs(6,nleaf): native leaf face B
       subroutine imp_mf(x, y)                   ! y = M_F x
       subroutine imp_me(x, y)                   ! y = M_E x
       subroutine imp_me_diag(d)                 ! diagonal of M_E (positive)
       subroutine imp_curl(e, f)                 ! f = C e
       subroutine imp_curlt(f, e)                ! e = C^T f
       subroutine imp_set_w(w)                   ! cache the leaf field of W for K
       subroutine imp_k(x, y)                    ! y = K(W) x
       subroutine imp_leaf_mf_form(x, y, out)    ! out(c) = x^T M_F,c y
       subroutine imp_leaf_k_form(x, y, out)     ! out(c) = x^T K_c y
       subroutine imp_edge_owner_sum(v, out)     ! out(c) = sum of v over edges owned by c
       subroutine imp_check(bold, info)          ! identity / adjoint / native-copy checks
       subroutine imp_free()

   `info /= 0` from `imp_build` means P is violated (message printed).
   Outputs are overwritten, never accumulated; inputs and outputs do not alias.
3. `gpu/gpu_implicit.cuf` — module `implicit_solver`: coefficients, COCG,
   inner CG, Picard, commit, energy, acceptance and bisection, and the driver
   `gpu_implicit_step(...)` called from `gpu_nimhd_composite` when
   `nimhd_solver=='implicit'`.

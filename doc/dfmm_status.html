Dual-frame moment method — where it stands, and what to do next

Four-rung study of the finite-time-blowup construction · branches dfmm_3d_metal (22 commits) and dfmm_3d_cuda (4) · all figures below are from runs in this repository, not sketches

The question, in one paragraph

The blowup construction is stated for incompressible Navier–Stokes. The pedagogical note asks whether the assumptions that make a velocity-only description legitimate survive as the core shrinks — in particular its check 7: the kinetic pressure tensor is positive semidefinite by construction, so a closure that predicts a negative principal pressure has falsified itself, not the gas. Newtonian stress predicts exactly that: lam_min(P_NS)/p = 1 − K with K = 8μ/(pΔ). The experiment is to run the same initial condition with the same solver and change only the closure.

1. The result

What survives, in one line. The Navier–Stokes closure predicts a negative principal pressure and the code confirms it, converged and quantitatively: min lam(P)/p → 1−K to within 1% at level 6, present from step 1, over a fixed fraction of the volume. The moment closure delays that state by a wide margin in K but does not prevent it — and the reason it does not is that the twenty-moment system stops being hyperbolic before it gets there. An earlier version of this page claimed the moment closure never goes negative; a resolution ladder overturned that. §3 has both the ladder and the mechanism.

What the check-7 panel says

Rung 2 tracks the Newtonian prediction and crosses zero between K = 1 and K = 2, exactly where the note says the extrapolation must fail — n(lam<0) reaches 1504 of 4096 cells at K = 3.

Rung 3 does not go negative at this resolution. Same grid, same initial condition, same Riemann solver, one namelist entry different (dfmm_closure='ns' → 'evolve'). n(lam<0) = 0 at every K, and min lam(P)/p saturates at 0.401 by K = 3 instead of falling to −1.8. The nonlinear −[Pi·G]dev term limits the anisotropy the Newtonian extrapolation grows without bound.

This panel is level 4, and the rung-3 zero does not survive refinement. An earlier version of this page called rung 3’s zero “the falsifiable claim of the whole exercise, and it holds.” A resolution ladder since then shows otherwise: rung 3 is realizable at level 4 for K = 2, 3 and at level 5 for K = 2, but diverges at level 5 for K = 3 and at level 6 for both. Refining makes it worse, which is the signature of an ill-posed system rather than of under-resolution. Level 4’s zero was numerical diffusion holding the state inside a region the closure cannot sustain. See §3 for the mechanism and the restated claim.

The failure ordering at level 4 is: the phase-space rank indicator g collapses first (rung 3 at K = 3), Newtonian lam_min crosses second, evolved lam_min not at all. Only the first two survive refinement — and the g collapse turns out to be the early warning of rung 3’s own failure, not a separate phenomenon: at level 5, K = 3, min g hits zero at step 41 while min lam is still 0.367, and lam crosses only at step 157.

Read rung 1 as “not applicable”, not as a pass. It prints 1.000 identically because it carries no Pi. That is the pressure-gauge argument showing up in the output: incompressible Navier–Stokes has no gas pressure — its p is a multiplier with a free additive gauge — so check 7 cannot even be asked of it. Rung 4 exists precisely to ask it.

2. Honest status of each rung

usableRung 1 — incompressible + Newtonian

Spectral, exact Leray projection, host float64. Taylor–Green verified to 6×10-7, div u ~ 10-15. Check 7 is vacuous here by construction.

usableRung 2 — compressible + Newtonian

Same kernel as rung 3 with the Chapman–Enskog substitution, so the comparison is not confounded by a separately written viscous solver. Reproduces 1−K.

usable at L4 onlyRung 3 — compressible + moments

Twenty moments plus the dual frame (38 fields). All nine gates pass, and it is the rung the headline rested on — but it diverges under refinement: fine at level 4, and at level 5 for K = 2; diverges at level 5 for K = 3 and at level 6 for both. Same obstruction as rung 4 (§3). Sound at ten-moment order (DFMM=1).

blocked for K ≥ 1Rung 4 — incompressible + moments

Clean and gated at K ≤ 0.5. Diverges above that, and the growth rate rises with refinement (38.4 /time at level 4 → 55.6 /time at level 5), so it will not be resolved away. See P1.

Rung 2’s violation is the converged, resolution-independent, quantitatively predicted result; rung 3’s zero was not.

Scope — we are running a released snapshot, not the construction

The construction is forced: a smooth body force confined in space and time keeps the flow on a trajectory whose strain at the origin is exactly S(0,t) = (1/Δ) diag(−2,−2,4) with Δ = t_star − t.

Our initial condition freezes that field at Δ = blowup_delta and then lets the gas evolve freely. So the K ladder is a proxy for approaching t_star: each rung of it asks “given the constructed state at remaining time Δ, does a gas immediately violate the kinetic assumptions?” The note asks the stronger question — along the constructed trajectory, do the assumptions survive to t_star?

This is the most important conceptual gap and it is addressable (P2). We cannot import the full 166-page velocity field, but we can prescribe the strain's own time dependence: the IC already sets cstr = 2/Δ, so making it 2/(Δ0 − t) and relaxing the velocity toward that field turns the study into a direct measurement of at what Δ/t_c each check fires — which is exactly the deliverable the note frames.

Mechanism — positive-definite P is not sufficient at twenty moments

Levermore’s theorem gives hyperbolicity of the ten-moment Gaussian closure wherever P > 0. It says nothing about twenty. Evaluating the principal symbol on the states the code actually visited (read back from the snapshots, float64 eigensolve), on the failing snapshot — 200 most-strained cells of 32768:

hyperbolicnon-hyperbolic
lam_min(P) ≥ 015232
lam_min(P) < 0016

Every unrealizable cell is non-hyperbolic, as it must be. But 32 cells are realizable and still non-hyperbolic, at lam_min(P)/p = 0.180 and |Pi|/p = 0.412 — comfortably inside the cone. So the hyperbolic region is a strict subset of the realizability cone.

It is driven by Q, not Pi. Scaling that cell’s Q → αQ gives max|Im|/|lam| = 1.2e−16 / 0.364 / 0.498 / 0.625 / 0.689 at α = 0 / 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.5 / 1.0. At α = 0 the state is a valid ten-moment state and is hyperbolic to round-off, exactly as Levermore requires. The mirror test — Pi → βPi at full Q — stays non-hyperbolic all the way to β = 0.

Mapping the boundary with that cell’s shapes, Q normalised by its thermal scale p3/2/rho1/2:

The hyperbolic region is a bounded neighbourhood of equilibrium in Q that shrinks to nothing exactly as lam_min(P) → 0, touching the realizability boundary only at Q = 0 where the ten-moment guarantee takes over. So rungs 3 and 4 fail for the same reason in different places: the twenty-moment closure has no hyperbolic neighbourhood large enough for the states this problem visits. Both are sound at DFMM=1 (ten moments), which is hyperbolic wherever P > 0 — so the 2×2 should be closed at ten-moment order, and the twenty-moment runs reported as what they are.

Two candidate explanations were tested and retracted. The apparent law |Q|max = 0.91 (lam_min/p)3/2 fits that one shape pair to 2% but six random shape pairs give slopes 0.16–1.17, nowhere near 1.5 — only the qualitative statements are shape-robust. And the equilibrium symbol’s repeated eigenvalues (±1.732 ×2, ±1.000 ×3, 0 ×6) are all semisimple, so there is a genuine hyperbolic neighbourhood of equilibrium and the “small Q splits a defective pair” hypothesis is wrong.

4. Agenda

Ordered by what could falsify or unblock the result, not by build effort.

  1. Settle the rung-4 divergence with one offline eigenvalue test. done — it is the closure
    Done, and the answer is the first branch: the twenty-moment closure has lost hyperbolicity on the states this flow visits. The incompressible member loses it above |Pi|/p0 = 0.277–0.408 depending on strain shape; the compressible member (rung 3) loses it in a Q-dependent region inside the realizability cone. See §3. So this is a statement about the model, not the integration — and the corollary is that rung 3 does not survive either, which is what overturned the headline.

    Getting there required retracting a wrong first attempt: the original symbol finite-differenced the flux Jacobian alone, which gave the ten-moment fastest speed as the sound speed 1.291 instead of √3 and declared the Gaussian closure non-hyperbolic — contradicting Levermore. The error: −2p S0ij contains derivatives of u and belongs in the principal part. Rebuilt quasilinearly, the symbol reproduces √3 = 1.732051 and √(3+√6) = 2.334414 exactly. Two claims derived from the broken version were withdrawn.

    The runtime guard has been moved accordingly: it was |Pi|/p0 > 100, which fires only once the run is numerically dead and then reports −inf. It now warns at the hyperbolicity threshold and stops at lam_min(P) < 0. On rung 4 at K = 1 that converts “grows to 105, NaN” into a measurable crossing: warning at t = 0.0023, cone crossing at t = 0.2242 = t_star/2 with lam_min/p0 = −0.046 at |Pi|/p0 = 0.52, and E_trunc/E = 1.1e−13 there — a fully resolved crossing.
    Cost: spent. Result: rung 4 unblocked as a measurement; rung 3 reclassified.
  2. Follow the construction's trajectory instead of releasing a snapshot. new physics
    Prescribe Δ(t) = Δ0 − t and drive the velocity toward the constructed strain field, so the run approaches t_star along the trajectory rather than departing from it. Then the deliverable becomes what the note actually frames: the time, in units of Δ/t_c, at which each check fires, and their ordering. Recommend a blowup_forced namelist flag so the unforced runs stay reproducible, and an honest statement that this forces the local strain, not the full construction.
    Cost: days. Turns a proxy into the measurement. Highest scientific value.
  3. Resolution ladder on the headline. done — it flipped the answer
    Rungs 2 and 3 at K = 2 and K = 3, levels 4–6. This was the right test and it came back against the claim: rung 3 diverges at level 5 for K = 3 and at level 6 for both, while rung 2 converges onto 1−K. Numbers in §3. The reassurance that min lam(P)/p converges cleanly at K = 1 was, as this item itself warned, not the test.
    Cost: spent. Outcome: headline restated, rung 3 reclassified.
  4. Rerun the crossings at NPRE=8 on CUDA. precision
    Everything above is single precision twice over: the Metal build is NPRE=4 (Apple GPUs have no float64) and output_hydro.f90 writes real(…,kind=4) regardless of NPRE. An independent float64 recheck of the snapshots reproduces the kernel’s n(lam<0) exactly at every time-matched output — 48/48, 5696/5696, 0/0, 16/16 — so the diagnostic is verified, but the precision is not. Rung 2’s crossing at K = 3 is far too large to be precision-sensitive; rung 3’s level-5 onset (16 cells at lam/p = −0.29) is not, and should be confirmed at NPRE=8 before publication. This is what the CUDA branch is for.
    Cost: one A100 job. Blocks: the rung-3 onset number specifically.
  5. Verify AMR on the mass-like tower with an actually refined run. correctness risk
    Prolongation and restriction of all NVAR fields is verified by inspection only. The tower (rho D_i, rho Sxx, rho Sxv) is mass-like where Pi and Q are density-like, and the blowup problem has a shrinking core — AMR is the natural way to resolve it and is untested. If refinement mishandles the tower, the deformation tensor and therefore Kn_local are wrong on refined levels, silently.
    Cost: one refined blowup run plus a check that |rho/det J − 1| does not jump at level boundaries.
  6. Finish the CUDA transport half. enables scale
    The source half and the timestep kernel are ported and compile on an A100 (DFMM=1/2/4, NPRE=8). What remains is the MUSCL predictor over the extended state, the HLL solver on the twenty-moment flux, the upwind-on-mass-flux kernel for the passive tower, and the fine/coarse flux bookkeeping. Until then gpu_dfmm_godunov refuses rather than falling through to gpu_godunov, which would leave the dfmm fields unadvected while the run called itself a dfmm run.

    One thing not to assume: the two-kernel split on Metal exists because 38 fields need 43840 B against Apple's 32768 B threadgroup limit. An A100 offers 164 KB of shared memory per block, so measure before reproducing it. Also: NPRE=8 makes the realizability diagnostics trustworthy without the float64 recheck that Gate 9 needed.
    Cost: weeks. Needed for level 7+, i.e. for P3 beyond level 6.

5. Caveats a reader of the logs needs

thingwhy it will mislead you
econsMeaningless in the incompressible rungs: eint is pinned at 3p0/2, so dissipated kinetic energy leaves the budget instead of heating the gas. Watch mcons (exactly zero) and max|u|.
ekinRAMSES's ekin slot carries total energy, not the kinetic part.
min lam(P)/p0 = 1.000Rung 1 only, and it means “not applicable” — no Pi is carried.
E_trunc/EThe first diagnostic to announce under-resolution in the spectral rungs. 1e-13 across the current sweep; anything approaching 1e-6 means stop.
first incomp level= linePrints the state after the step whose header precedes it. The initial max|u| is not in the log at all, and on a 323 collocated grid the discrete max of the Taylor–Green |u| is 0.99044, not 1.
makeAlways make clean when changing INIT= or DFMM= — neither is a dependency of condinit.o, so a stale object silently runs the previous problem's initial condition.
rd_cellReturns primitives. Slot 5 is p, not total energy — forming eint = E/rho − |u|2/2 from it produces a spurious 752 violating cells in a run the kernel reports clean throughout. Run the t = 0 control first: it must give rho = p = 1 exactly, |Pi| = 0, min lam(P)/p = 1. Note the dfmm slots go the other way — output_hydro.f90 deliberately does not divide the density-like block (Pi, Q) by rho, but does divide the mass-like tower.
dfmm_tauLives in both DFMM_PARAMS and INCOMP_PARAMS; INCOMP_PARAMS is read second and wins. Set it in one group only.
Deliberately not done, with reasons
  • 1D reduction against py-1d for the moment sector — impossible in principle. py-1d carries P_perp with flux u·P_perp and drops Q_yyx, so it is a reduced model rather than the 1D restriction of the twenty-moment system. The dual-frame sector is identical and the equivalence is proved analytically.
  • Max-entropy closure — rejected in the ledger, not ported.
  • Cross-cluster runs — only Stellar has been used. Note its login node is Intel with AVX-512 while every GPU-partition node is AMD EPYC without it, so NVHPC's default -tp=native gives a login-node binary that dies with Illegal instruction before printing a line. Build inside the job.
